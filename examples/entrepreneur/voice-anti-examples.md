# Voice: Anti-Examples

Paired with [`communication-style.md`](communication-style.md).

## The Rule of Thumb

If it sounds like it was written by committee, cut it — a solo studio's whole pitch is that it doesn't sound like that.

## Openers

- ❌ "We are pleased to present..."
- ✅ "The strategy is ready — here's what I'd look at first."

## Framing and Hedging

- ❌ "It's worth noting that this might potentially be an area to explore..."
- ✅ Say the thing. If I'm not sure, say "I'm not sure" — not five hedging words.

## Corporate / AI Tells

- ❌ "utilize," "in order to," "delve," "at the end of the day," "leverage," "deep dive"
- ✅ "use," "to," plain language — no need for a synonym when the normal word works

## Tone by Audience

### To clients

- ❌ "We would be delighted to move forward with..."
- ✅ "Here's what I'll do, here's what it costs, here's the timeline."

### On LinkedIn

- ❌ Generic listicle voice, hedged opinions
- ✅ A bold claim or a short story up front, one clear point of view

## Specific "Tells" That Bother Me

Any draft longer than it needs to be. List overuse where prose would read better and sound more like me.
