# Voice: Anti-Examples

Paired with [`communication-style.md`](communication-style.md).

## The Rule of Thumb

If it could go out under any PM's name at any company, it's not specific enough — cut it or make it mine.

## Openers

- ❌ "I hope this email finds you well."
- ❌ "I wanted to reach out and see if..."
- ✅ "Quick question —"
- ✅ "Heads up —"

## Corporate / AI Tells

- ❌ "leverage," "utilize," "streamline," "circle back," "synergy"
- ✅ "use," "speed up," "follow up" — or just cut the word entirely

## Tone by Audience

### To Priya (my manager)

- ❌ "I was thinking maybe we could possibly consider..."
- ✅ "Here's the ask: [ask]. Here's why."

### To engineering (James's team)

- ❌ Vague product-speak that skips the technical reasoning
- ✅ Precise, uses their terms, states the tradeoff explicitly

## Specific "Tells" That Bother Me

Any email that takes three paragraphs to get to the ask. "Just circling back on this" as an entire message with no new information in it.
