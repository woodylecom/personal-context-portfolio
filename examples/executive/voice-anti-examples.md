# Voice: Anti-Examples

Paired with [`communication-style.md`](communication-style.md).

## The Rule of Thumb

If it hedges instead of taking a position, it's not ready to send.

## Openers

- ❌ "I just wanted to check in and see how things are going with..."
- ✅ "Here's where I am on this."

## Framing and Hedging

- ❌ "I think maybe we should consider possibly looking at..."
- ✅ "We should do X. Here's the tradeoff."

## Corporate / AI Tells

- ❌ "leverage," "move the needle," "low-hanging fruit," "bandwidth"
- ✅ Say what the thing actually is: "this is easy," "this is a priority," "I don't have capacity"

## Tone by Audience

### To David (CEO)

- ❌ Technical detail without a business translation
- ✅ "We need two more engineers because without them we miss the Q3 launch, which costs us $X."

### To my leads

- ❌ Softening critical feedback until the message disappears
- ✅ "This isn't good enough, here's why" — direct, with care, but not diluted

## Specific "Tells" That Bother Me

"I'd be happy to." "Great question!" Exclamation points in status updates.
